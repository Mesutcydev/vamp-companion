Type & rhythm

SF Pro for controls and copy. Barlow Condensed Black for the VAMP wordmark and clear SF Pro Semibold for UI headings and buttons. SF Mono for pairing codes and addresses. Page titles are 25 pt, body copy 14 pt, captions 13 pt, and buttons 14 pt. Use a 4 pt spacing grid, 24 pt page insets, 18 pt card insets, and 8 pt panel corners, subtle neutral borders, and flat control surfaces.

Behavior & voice

Navigation is immediate. Press feedback is subtle and respects Reduce Motion. Text has no decorative shadow. Models use informative full-width rows. Color supports a text label; it never carries status alone. Keep instructions short and actions concrete. Use “Pair your iPhone,” “Download,” and “iPhone access ready.”

Assets

Vector mark · Vector app icon · 1024 px PNG · Design tokens

Keep the mark’s proportions and the space around it. Use the red signal only for Companion’s identity. Statuses use clear labels and symbols. Red marks a primary action, selection, or a state needing attention. The icon export script writes only Companion’s separate Mac asset catalog.
