Type & rhythm

SF Pro for controls and copy. SF Pro Rounded Heavy for the VAMP wordmark. SF Mono for pairing codes and addresses. Page titles are 25 pt, body copy 13 pt, captions 11 pt. Use a 4 pt spacing grid, 24 pt page insets, 18 pt card insets, and 16 pt card corners.

Behavior & voice

Navigation is immediate. Press feedback is subtle and respects Reduce Motion. Color supports a text label; it never carries status alone. Keep instructions short and actions concrete. Use “Pair your iPhone,” “Download,” and “iPhone access ready.”
